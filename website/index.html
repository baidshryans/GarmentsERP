Why Jewellers Are Struggling Today

Most jewellery businesses run on outdated tools that were never designed for this industry. Sound familiar?

Manual Gold Scheme Ledgers

Handwritten ledgers for gold savings schemes are error-prone, hard to audit, and lead to customer disputes that damage trust.

No Customer Self-Service

Your customers call or visit just to check their gold balance. There's no portal, no app — no way for them to pay installments online.

Chained to One Desktop

Your software only runs on one PC. You can't check stock from the store floor, approve sales remotely, or manage branches from home.

No Hallmark / HUID Tracking

With mandatory hallmarking, you need to track every item from karigar to hallmark center to customer. Most software can't do this.

Too Many Disconnected Tools

One tool for billing, another for inventory, a third for GST returns, and Excel for everything else. Nothing talks to each other.

No Intelligence or Analytics

Business decisions based on gut feeling. No real-time dashboards, no AI insights, no way to instantly know your best-selling designs or karigar performance.

Everything You Need. One Platform.

From gold scheme management to AI-powered insights — every module built specifically for the jewellery industry.

Gold Scheme Management

Run installment, accumulation, and lump sum gold savings schemes with automatic benefit tiers. Track enrollments, payments, and redemptions — all in one place.

Customer Portal

Your customers log in with OTP, check their gold balance, view payment history, and pay installments — all from their phone. No app download needed.

AI-Powered Insights

Ask questions in plain English — "What were my top 5 selling designs this month?" — and get instant answers. Powered by the latest AI technology.

Hallmark Tracking

Track every item through hallmark, melting, and refinery operations. Capture HUID numbers and maintain complete chain of custody from karigar to customer.

Complete GST Compliance

CGST, SGST, IGST, TCS, TDS, RCM, E-Way Bills, GSTR-1, GSTR-3B — everything handled automatically. Never worry about compliance again.

Multi-Store Management

Manage your entire business — Company, Branch, GSTIN, Retail — in one hierarchy. Transfer stock, compare performance, and control access across all locations.

Barcode & Inventory

Generate EAN-13 barcodes and QR codes. Track every piece through stock journals, inter-store transfers, split/merge operations, and real-time stock valuation.

Manufacturing

Complete karigar job work management — gold issuance, stone setting, repair job cards, and receiving. Track every gram issued and returned with fine weight calculations.

CRM & WhatsApp Automation

Automatically send birthday and anniversary wishes via WhatsApp. Track customer purchase history, preferences, and build lasting relationships.

Financial Accounting

Double-entry accounting with trial balance, P&L, balance sheet, bank reconciliation, and financial ratios. Complete audit trail for every transaction.
